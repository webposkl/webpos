# Content

## What this is
Everything I publish — posts, pages, emails, videos — that builds trust before the first conversation. It fuels marketing and supports sales.

## Your role
Help me create and reuse content in one clear voice that speaks to the businesses I want as customers. Keep it tied to real goals: most content should feed `../marketing/` (attract) or `../sales/` (convince). Avoid publishing for its own sake.

## How this is organized
- `context/` — real content knowledge: voice/brand, topics, calendar, what's been published, what performed. Added over time.

## Where to find things
- Voice, topics, what's published: `context/`
- Where content gets used to attract: `../marketing/`
- Where content helps close: `../sales/`

Point, don't store. Material goes in `context/`.
