# North Star

I run a point-of-sale business. I sell POS systems — the hardware and software — to businesses that need a reliable way to take payments and run what they sell.

## Where I'm headed
A steady flow of the right customers, and a business that runs on systems instead of depending on me to hold every piece together.

## Right now
Two things matter more than everything else combined:

1. **Get the website live.** It is the front door. Everything else points to it.
2. **Get leads coming in.** A repeatable way for the right businesses to find me and raise their hand.

If a task doesn't move one of these two forward, it can usually wait.

## What I care about
- **Marketing** that brings the right people in, not just more people.
- **Sales** that turns interest into paying customers without pressure.
- **Finances** I actually understand and stay in control of.
- **Content** that builds trust before the first conversation.
- **Health**, because none of this works if I burn out.

Everything in this folder exists to serve this.
