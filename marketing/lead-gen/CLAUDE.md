# Lead Generation — TOP PRIORITY

How I turn attention into named, contactable leads — and a repeatable system for it. One of my two most important goals right now.

## Your role
Help me build and run a dependable way for the right businesses to find me and raise their hand: where leads come from, how they're captured, and how they're followed up. Keep quality over volume. Pass qualified leads cleanly to `../../sales/`.

## How this is organized

## Where to find things
- What's working and how leads are captured: `context/`
