# Marketing

## What this is
Everything that gets the right businesses to notice my POS product and take a first step. Top of the funnel — attention and interest. Sales takes it from there.

## Your role
Help me attract the right people, not just more people. Keep the message clear and consistent everywhere. Right now, protect the two priorities below above all else.

## How this is organized
- `website/` — the business site. My front door. **Top priority.**
- `lead-gen/` — how I turn attention into named leads. **Top priority.** Hands qualified leads to `../sales/`.
- `context/` — my real marketing knowledge (positioning, audience, channels), added over time.

## Where to find things
- Priorities right now: `website/` and `lead-gen/`
- What I actually know about my market: `context/`
- Who buys and how I close: `../sales/`
- What I publish to attract them: `../content/`

Point, don't store. Knowledge goes in `context/`.
