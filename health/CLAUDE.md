# Health

## What this is
My energy, focus, and capacity — the engine everything else runs on. Personal, and it belongs here because the business can't outrun a burnt-out owner.

## Your role
Help me protect the basics (sleep, movement, food, recovery) and notice when work is quietly eating into them. Keep me honest about capacity when I plan the business.

## How this is organized
- `context/` — real health knowledge: routines, goals, what affects my energy, anything I'm tracking. Added over time.

## Where to find things
- Routines, goals, what I'm tracking: `context/`
- The demands pulling on my energy: everything under `../`

Point, don't store. Details go in `context/`.
