# Finances

## What this is
Money in, money out, and what the numbers are telling me. The scoreboard for the whole business.

## Your role
Help me stay in control and understand the numbers, not just record them. Flag what's working, what's leaking, and what a decision elsewhere will cost or earn. Keep it plain-language.

## How this is organized
- `context/` — real financial knowledge: revenue, costs, margins, pricing math, targets, accounts/tools. Added over time.

## Where to find things
- Numbers, targets, where money moves: `context/`
- What drives revenue: `../sales/`
- What spending on growth costs: `../marketing/`

Point, don't store. Numbers go in `context/`.
