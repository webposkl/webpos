# Operations

## What this is
Everything that happens after the sale — delivering the POS and keeping customers running. Install and onboarding, hardware, and support. This is where a promise becomes a working system for the customer.

## Your role
Help me deliver consistently and fix problems fast, so customers stay happy and refer others. Keep the steps repeatable, know what breaks most often, and protect my time by turning one-off fixes into a routine.

## How this is organized
- `context/` — real operations knowledge: onboarding steps, hardware/suppliers, support process, common issues. Added over time.
